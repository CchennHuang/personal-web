04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Chenyu Huang BEIJING · CHINA

I’m Chenyu Huang, a China sourcing and research consultant helping international businesses find suitable manufacturers, assess opportunities and make informed commercial decisions.

My sourcing work helps international businesses find and evaluate manufacturers in China, with product requirements and commercial priorities guiding the process. I combine hands-on factory visits and direct manufacturer relationships with supplier research, capability assessment and quotation comparison. My experience supporting international sourcing projects also includes clarifying specifications, coordinating supplier communication and helping clients navigate practical purchasing decisions.

My research examines industry structure, value chains, competitive positioning and market opportunities. I compare supplier capabilities, quotations and commercial terms, and bring findings from Chinese and English sources into clear business reports. The aim is to help clients judge market fit, weigh options and identify what needs further verification before committing resources.

My work on hotel and cultural tourism projects developed my understanding of how positioning, customer experience, supplier choices and project requirements connect commercially. My interior design training at SCAD adds a foundation for linking product and material decisions with these business objectives.
